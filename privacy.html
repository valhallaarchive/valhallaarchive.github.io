Privacy Policy for VALHALLA ARCHIVE

Last Updated: October 9, 2026  Effective Date: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VALHALLA ARCHIVE ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and deletes information when you play our PC and mobile game VALHALLA ARCHIVE: CCG (the "Game") and use related services. VALHALLA ARCHIVE is an independent game studio operated as a sole proprietorship in the Republic of Korea.

1. Information We Collect

Account and platform identifiers

  • Your platform user ID (Steam ID or Google Play Games ID), provided by the platform when you sign in.
  • The in-game account ID assigned by our backend, and the display name and profile settings you choose in the Game.

Game data

  • Card collection, decks, match records, rankings, progress, in-game currency balances, mail, and cosmetic selections.

Purchase data

  • Records of in-game purchases: the product purchased, order ID, price, time, and status (completed, cancelled, or refunded).
  • We do not receive or store your payment card number, bank details, or billing address. Payments are processed entirely by the platform you buy through (Steam or Google Play).
  • On Android, the in-app purchase library we use (Unity In-App Purchasing) sends purchase event data to Unity Technologies while processing a purchase: the product purchased, the store order ID and purchase receipt, device model, OS version and language, app version, and an installation identifier. It does not send advertising identifiers.

Technical data

  • IP address, approximate country or region derived from it, and login timestamps. These are recorded automatically by our backend provider when you connect.
  • Game version and platform (PC or Android).
  • Device model, operating system version, and a device identifier, which our backend provider may record when you sign in.

Support communications

  • Your email address and the content of your message when you contact us.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your real name, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.
  • The Game contains no advertising SDKs. We do not use any analytics SDK of our own; the only exception is the purchase event data that the in-app purchase library described above sends to its provider.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above only to:

  • Operate the Game and keep your account synchronized across platforms.
  • Deliver purchased items and handle cancellations, refunds, and purchase limits.
  • Run competitive features such as rankings, leagues, and asynchronous PvP matches (your display name, profile, and deck may be shown to other players in these features).
  • Prevent fraud, cheating, and abuse, and keep the service secure.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Legal bases (for users in the EEA and UK): performance of our contract with you (providing the Game), our legitimate interests (security and fraud prevention), and compliance with legal obligations.

4. International Data Transfers

Our backend provider stores and processes data on servers located outside the Republic of Korea, including in the United States. By using the Game, your information is transferred to and processed in those locations. We rely on our provider's contractual and technical safeguards for these transfers. On Android, the purchase event data described in Section 1 is also sent to Unity Technologies, which processes it on servers outside the Republic of Korea, including in the United States.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

  • Retention: We keep your account and game data for as long as your account exists.
  • In-game deletion: You can request account deletion at any time from the in-game Settings menu.
  • 30-day grace period: After a deletion request, your account enters a 30-day grace period. Signing in during this period lets you cancel the request.
  • Permanent deletion: After the grace period, your account identifiers, card collection, match history, and progress are permanently and irreversibly deleted from our backend.
  • Deletion by email: You can also request deletion of your account and data by emailing valhallaarchiveofficial@gmail.com.
  • Legal retention exceptions: Where the law requires certain records to be kept, they are stored separately and only for the required period, then deleted. Under Korean law this includes records of contracts and payments (5 years), records of consumer complaints and dispute handling (3 years), and service access logs (3 months). Purchase records held by Steam or Google Play are governed by their own policies.
  • Abuse-prevention record: When you claim a platform DLC pack in the game, we keep a minimal record linking your platform account identifier (e.g., Steam ID) with your game account ID and the claim date. This record is used only to prevent the same purchase from being claimed more than once, and for no other purpose. It is retained even after you delete your game account, for as long as the service is operated, and is destroyed when the service ends.
  • Purchases and deletion: Deleting your account permanently removes unused in-game currency and items. They cannot be restored.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at valhallaarchiveofficial@gmail.com. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 14 (or the minimum age required in your country, if higher). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Game data and purchases are processed on servers, not on your device, and access to the backend is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users and authorities of a breach where the law requires it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be announced on this page and, where appropriate, in the Game at least 7 days before they take effect. The "Last Updated" date above shows the latest revision.

기술 정보

  • IP 주소, IP에서 추정한 국가·지역, 접속 일시(접속 시 백엔드 제공자가 자동 기록)

  • 로그인 시 백엔드 제공자가 기록할 수 있는 기기 모델, 운영체제 버전, 기기 식별자

  • 문의 시 이용자의 이메일 주소와 문의 내용

수집하지 않는 정보

  • 실명, 전화번호, 정확한 위치, 연락처, 사진, 광고 식별자는 수집하지 않습니다.
